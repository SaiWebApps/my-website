## Direction contract

THESIS: Nothing is hidden. Like a Coptic binding whose chain stitch runs on the outside of the spine, this site shows its structure: every page is a signature sewn to one visible spine. That is also my engineering creed. Crucible makes agents show their work, and GraphRAG evaluation checks every generated fact against the graph. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: oak boards (#8A5A34) and walnut (#5B3A22) as the dominant ground, undyed linen thread (#D9CBB0) for the chain stitch, and rag paper pages (#F2EEE6) held inside the boards. Blind tooling (debossed shapes, no ink) is the only ornament. Type is a book face (Spectral or Libre Caslon Text) justified with real hyphenation, small caps for running heads, and a sans for captions.

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - Each page is a signature. **Services:** one leaf per offer. **Career:** one leaf per chapter, with the Wharton letters tipped in as plates.
- **Speaking**, **Research** and **Projects** each a signature. **Contact:** the colophon, with booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the closed book on the oak boards, its chain stitch running visibly down the spine at left. The headline is blind-tooled into the cover. Opening it (click, or once on load) reveals the first signature. Signature interaction: sewing and turning. Each section is a signature you open along the spine, and the stitch draws as you move between sections.
