## Direction contract

THESIS: A career is layered like posters on a city hoarding. Tear back today's poster (Apple) and Amazon is underneath, then SIG, Microsoft and Carnegie Mellon. The ragged edges show sixteen years of depth at once. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: a paste-gray backing board (#B9B6AE), sun-bleached poster inks (faded red #D46A5A, faded blue #6F8FB3, yellow #E8C35A), fibrous tear edges (#EDE6D6) and black ink. Each era's poster has its own type system at its own scale (a slab, a condensed grotesk, a Bodoni, a stencil), and every fact on a revealed poster is complete and readable. Tear edges are generated (canvas or SVG displacement), never polygon clip-paths.

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Career:** the full hoarding, torn era by era.
- **Services:** a fresh paste-up, where each offer is a poster pasted over the last.
- **Speaking:** the Eagles keynote as the event poster, plus the program flyers. **Research:** four bills.
- **Projects:** five posters. **Contact:** a fresh bill with booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the newest poster, "Grow the people. The platforms follow.", with my name and title, pasted over older ones. One corner is already torn, showing an Alexa-era poster beneath. Signature interaction: drag or tap to tear. A tear reveals the poster below with a paper-fiber edge (canvas), and it is keyboard operable through a "previous layer" control in the world's vocabulary.
