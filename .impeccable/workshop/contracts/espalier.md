## Direction contract

THESIS: An espalier is a tree trained flat along wires on a warm wall, so it fruits where you planned. That is how I grow people: the platform is the trellis, the people are the branches, and the platforms follow as fruit. Seasons pass between pages. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: a warm brick wall (#A8553D, mortar #D9CFC2), galvanized wires (#9AA3A8), and a pear espalier with bark (#5C4433), leaf green (#4E7A3A), blossom (#FAF7F2) and pear gold (#D8B44A). Zinc plant tags hang from the wires. Type is stamped zinc tags (Courier Prime) with a botanical display face (Gloock or Young Serif) and a quiet sans for prose (Albert Sans). No cream page: the brick wall is the ground.

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Services:** each offer is a zinc tag on its own wire (name, price, length), with the abstract on the tag's back when flipped.
- **Career:** each tier is an era. The trunk is Carnegie Mellon; Apple's tier is in full fruit.
- **Speaking:** a harvest-table season. **Research:** four grafts. **Projects:** five young trees in pots.
- **Contact:** winter: bare wire, a single tag, booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the wall at golden hour with a candelabra espalier trained along the wires. Its branches grow in on load, eight young branches on the newest tier, five of them thicker (the senior engineers), and blossom opens. "Grow the people. The platforms follow." is chalked on the brick. Signature interaction: growth. Scroll grows branches along the wires (DrawSVG), and seasons cross-fade per page.
