## Direction contract

THESIS: A mid-century film title sequence: one flat saturated field per act, cut-paper shapes, and a single jittering line that builds meaning across timed cards. One idea per card, which is exactly the one-subject-per-screen taste. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: a field per act (burnt orange #E2591F, mustard #E7B124, teal #1C7C7D, red #C8312B, bone #EFE8DA) with ink-black (#121212) cut-paper silhouettes and no faces ever. Type is skinny hand-set caps sitting slightly off baseline (Smooch Sans or a condensed face with per-letter jitter), set in hard asymmetry.

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Services:** a card per offer, each with its own cut-paper symbol, and the field color changes by group.
- **Speaking:** the Eagles keynote as the main title, with programs as the supporting credits, each a timed card.
- **Career:** one act per chapter. **Research:** four title cards.
- **Projects:** five. **Contact:** the end card, with booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a black cut-paper line enters a burnt-orange field and branches. Each branch becomes a small figure (the people), and the figures lift a bar that becomes a platform as "Grow the people. The platforms follow." cuts in, card by card. Signature interaction: scroll-timed cards with a jittering line. The line is one continuous SVG path (MorphSVG and DrawSVG) carried across cards and across pages (view transitions keep it).

FORM: catalog challenger posters-covers-sleeves-saul-bass-title in round 3, verdict competitive; seed key 6d3d75b1 --reroll 2.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
