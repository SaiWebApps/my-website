## Direction contract

THESIS: In football, a coaching tree shows which assistants grew into head coaches. This site is my leadership coaching tree: the people I grew are the branches, and the platforms they shipped hang from them. "Grow the people. The platforms follow." becomes a diagram you can walk. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: a drenched practice-field green (#123F2C), chalk-white lines (#EEF0E8), and the broadcast first-down yellow (#F2D22E) as the single moving highlight, with field-paint white numerals at true field scale. Type is an athletic condensed face (Big Shoulders Display) for names and headlines, with a clean grotesk (Schibsted Grotesk) for prose. Chalk texture is grain on the lines only. No team branding, no mascots, no Eagles colors.

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Services:** a playbook. Each offer is one play diagram drawn in chalk, showing the engagement's motion in X/O language (you bring the design, we review, you leave with a recommendation). The three groups are the playbook's sections. Plays swap in place with Flip.
- **Speaking:** the stadium. The Eagles keynote is a field-level moment of 300+ seats igniting, and each Praxtera engagement is a "film session" chalk card on the sideline.
- **Career:** the full tree with each chapter as a branch. Selecting a branch zooms the camera to it, and the branch reveals its bullets as notes along the line. The Wharton letters hang as framed awards at the Wharton branch.
- **Research:** four papers as four "theories of the game", each on its own field-length screen with its diagram.
- **Projects:** the plays I'm running now. Each venture is a live play.
- **Contact:** the huddle: one circle of chalk, with book, email and résumé.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the tree draws itself in chalk from one root node (me) up the screen: the Amazon branch with eight nodes, four of which ignite as "promoted" (half the team), and the Apple branch with eight nodes, five ringed as senior. The yellow line sweeps across as the headline lands, set huge on the field. Under the headline sit the name, the title, and two plays: "Services" and "Résumé". Signature interaction: the camera travels the tree. Every page is a branch of one continuous drawing, and cross-page view transitions keep the tree in place while the camera moves to that page's branch.
