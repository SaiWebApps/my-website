## Direction contract

THESIS: Executives meet big ideas on a stage: dark house, one slide, one sentence, one lit object. Every page is an act in a keynote I am giving, set on an LED wall in a stage built from light (Es Devlin, Apple events, Lincoln Financial Field). Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: a stage built from light, not a black page. Haze gradients, light cones from rigging, a cool LED wall white (#E8F0FF), warm tungsten (#FFB46A) from the wings, and the house in deep blue-black (#0A0C12) with seat rows catching light. Type is one confident grotesk at slide scale (Geologica or Onest bold). The slide is the only flat thing in a volumetric room.

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Services:** "one more thing" slides, one offer per slide build, with the price revealed as the build's final beat.
- **Speaking:** the Eagles keynote rebuilt as the opening act, followed by each program as its own act.
- **Career:** the story as a keynote narrative, one chapter per act, with the recommendations as audience quotes projected large.
- **Research:** four papers as four talks.
- **Projects:** a demo segment.
- **Contact:** the closing slide, which is the booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the stage in perspective, with the LED wall showing one sentence, "Grow the people. The platforms follow." A light column falls center stage. The house below is full: 300 seats picked out by light, the Eagles keynote audience. Signature interaction: slide builds. Each scroll step is a Magic Move-style build where words and shapes morph from one slide to the next (GSAP Flip and SplitText), the camera dollies slightly, and the light changes per act.
