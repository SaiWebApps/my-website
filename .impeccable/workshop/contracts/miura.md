## Direction contract

THESIS: A Miura-ori sheet deploys a large precise field from a hand-sized packet in one pull. That is the shape of my work: small inputs, large deployed systems. The engagement ladder is literal: the bigger the engagement, the more of the sheet deploys. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: a matte white sheet (#F3F4F2) and a gold foil packet (#C8A24A). Mountain creases are warm gray (#8C857B), valley creases pale blue (#9CC2E5), and ink is #15171A. A parallelogram tessellation rules every block. Type is an aerospace-spec grotesk (Saira or Barlow) with crease IDs in mono (JetBrains Mono).

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Services:** the Architecture Call deploys one panel, Design Review four, Audit, ROI and Evals more, and the retainer and Fractional CTO the full sheet. This is a truthful map of scope; each state shows one offer.
- **Career:** each chapter is a panel sequence that deploys with time.
- **Speaking:** the keynote is a full deploy. **Research:** four sheets. **Projects:** five packets.
- **Contact:** the sheet folds back into the packet, with booking on its face.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the gold packet, book-sized, with the headline foil-stamped on it. One pull (click or drag, or it happens once on load) deploys it into the full tessellated sheet, and each panel holds one fact, lit in turn. Signature interaction: the fold. A three.js or CSS 3D Miura fold opens and closes between pages, and each page is a different deployed state of the same sheet.
