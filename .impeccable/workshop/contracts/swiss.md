## Direction contract

THESIS: Each page is one commanding poster in the International Typographic Style. The grid does the persuading: one idea, one geometric figure, type as image. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: a committed field color per page (Home signal red #E1251B; Services cobalt #1F3FBF; Speaking deep orange #F26A1B; Career black #141414 with white; Research olive #7A8B2D; Projects violet #5E3AA8; Contact white), with black and white throughout. The grid is visible only through alignment. Type is a neo-grotesk at heavy weights and monumental sizes (Schibsted Grotesk or Hanken Grotesk), set ragged with real typographic rhythm. The figures are Müller-Brockmann geometry: arcs, circles and stepped bars built from the facts.

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Services:** a poster per offer, each with its own geometric figure built from that offer's length and scope, set as a series you move through like posters on a rail.
- **Career:** one poster per chapter. **Speaking:** a concert poster for the Eagles keynote.
- **Research:** four posters. **Projects:** five. **Contact:** a white poster with booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the red poster. "Grow the people." and "The platforms follow." are set as two monumental lines, with arcs that grow from small to large across the grid (people to platforms), plus the name, title and two doors. Signature interaction: the grid moves. On load and on scroll the poster's geometry assembles and the type behaves as matter, letters settling into place, readable at every frame. Pages change by sliding to the next poster in the series.
