## Direction contract

THESIS: Engineering leaders decide at the whiteboard. Every page is a design review in progress: the architecture of what I built, drawn live in marker, with the decisions written beside the boxes. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: a whiteboard surface (#F7F8F6) with ghosting from erased marks (#E3E5E2) and an aluminum tray frame (#B8BBBF). Markers are black (#1C1C1E), blue (#2357C6), red (#D7332B) and green (#1E8C4E), with magnets holding real artifacts. Type is a marker hand for diagram labels (Gochi Hand or Permanent Marker, used sparingly) with a clean grotesk for readable copy (Albert Sans). Diagrams are real architectures from the résumé, never decoration.

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Career:** each chapter is its system's diagram:
  - Apple: 15+ sources to the canonical model to search, recommendations and discovery for 100M+ users; the Spark batch to Flink/Kafka/Cassandra migration (60 hours to one minute); Crucible.
  - Alexa: GraphQL aggregation, $40M a year, and onboarding cut from two weeks to 15 minutes.
  - Microsoft: 4x power.
  - Recommendations are notes pinned under magnets.
- **Services:** each offer as the session whiteboard (what we would draw in that engagement).
- **Speaking:** the workshop board for HP, Morgan Lewis, PBS and Sonova, drawn from the session parts in speaking.ts.
- **Research:** each paper's model drawn as a diagram. **Projects:** each venture's system diagram.
- **Contact:** an empty board corner that reads "Book a call".

FIRST VIEWPORT: the board with one diagram drawing itself (DrawSVG): "people" boxes on the left grow arrows into "platform" boxes on the right, and the headline is written above it in marker. The name and title sit at top left, and Services and Résumé are two magnets. Signature interaction: diagrams draw, and you can redraw them. Hovering or focusing a box highlights its path; clicking a box zooms the board onto that part of the diagram.
