## Direction contract

THESIS: A Japanese CD sleeve: an almost-empty face holding one geometric mark, wrapped in a paper obi band crammed with credits. On every page the face holds the one vivid subject and the band carries every dense fact, so nothing is dropped and the screen never becomes a list. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: a white sleeve face (#FBFBFA) on paper gray (#D9D7D2), an obi red band (#E4004F) with risograph red (#FF2A5B) grain, and ink (#111111). Type is an ultra-condensed display (League Gothic or Six Caps) set vertically and huge, with a small mono (Spline Sans Mono) for band credits. The band's density against the face's emptiness is the whole design.

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - Every page is one sleeve with one mark. Services, Speaking, Career, Research, Projects and Contact are each a sleeve.
- **Services:** each offer is its own sleeve with its own mark (a 60-minute arc for the Call, a three-month ring for Fractional CTO). Its band holds the price, length, abstract and proof. You flip sleeves in a crate, one at a time.
- **Career:** each chapter is a sleeve with its band. The Wharton letters are the inserts that slide out of the sleeve.

FIRST VIEWPORT: one red geometric mark centered low on the white face: a circle with a growing segment. The band wraps the bottom third, packed with credits: roles, dates, offices, numbers, and the headline in vertical condensed type. Signature interaction: the band slides. Navigating slides the obi off one sleeve and onto the next, and pulling the band reveals what it covered.

FORM: catalog challenger posters-covers-sleeves-shibuya-boutique-sleeve in round 1, verdict competitive; seed key 6d3d75b1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
