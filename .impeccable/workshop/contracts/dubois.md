## Direction contract

THESIS: W.E.B. Du Bois's 1900 data portraits turned hard statistics into bold, hand-inked graphic argument, and each chart invented the form its question needed. My proof is quantitative, so every page is one data portrait: one invented chart, one true number, one argument. It is never a stat grid. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: - An aged board ground (#DCC6A0, pushed warmer than cream, with visible paper grain). Flat saturated inks: crimson (#D2273C), gold (#F2B33D), emerald (#2E8B57), royal blue (#3F5EB5), pink (#E7A0B0) and ink black (#141414).
- Type is hand-lettered small-caps captions (a caps grotesk with slight irregularity, such as Gantari or Familjen Grotesk) and a plain text face for prose.
- Charts are drawn in SVG, each a different form: spiral, wrapped bar, stepped squares, fan, concentric rings.

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Home:** one plate per screen: 8 engineers with 5 senior as stepped squares; $40M a year as a wrapped bar; 100M+ users as a fan; 300+ leaders as concentric rings.
- **Services:** the price ladder as stepped squares by price, one offer per plate.
- **Career:** a spiral timeline from 2010 to 2026, one chapter per turn. Two weeks to 15 minutes, 40 to 10 weekly issues, 4x power, 30% and 45% each get their own plate.
- **Speaking:** 300+ and 105+ students as forms. **Research:** four plates. **Projects:** five plates.
- **Contact:** a plate, with booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a Du Bois spiral of 60 hours collapsing to one minute (the Flink migration), drawn in crimson on board. "Grow the people. The platforms follow." is hand-lettered as the plate title, with name, title and the two doors. Signature interaction: ink lays down. Each chart draws its marks in sequence as you arrive, with flat fills wiping in. Hovering or focusing a mark lifts its exact value.

FORM: catalog challenger dubois-data-portraits in round 6, verdict wins; seed key 6d3d75b1 --reroll 5.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
