## Direction contract

THESIS: I built the graph behind Apple Music Credits, a system whose whole job is to credit the people who made the work. The site is a record and its liner notes: the record is the vivid object, and the notes credit the people and the platforms. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: a Reid Miles era jazz label's graphic system: a cobalt field (#0B3D91), white, and one hot yellow (#F5C518), with giant condensed type cropped hard off the edges and duotone shapes. The record itself is the black object, rendered in WebGL or CSS 3D with real groove sheen. Type is a heavy condensed display (Sofia Sans Extra Condensed) cropped off-canvas, with an italic text face for liner prose (Libre Caslon Text).

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Services:** the record's grooves are the offers. Side A is Lead and advise, Side B is Engagements, and a 7-inch single is Speaking and teaching. Drop the needle on a band and its sleeve-back panel slides out with name, length (like a track time), price, abstract and proof.
- **Speaking:** "Live at Lincoln Financial Field" is a live-album sleeve for the Eagles keynote, with the 300+ as a crowd of dots. Praxtera sessions are a tour poster set as one typographic broadside.
- **Career:** the gatefold booklet, one spread per chapter. Each spread has "Recorded at" (location), "Personnel" (team counts, never names), "Produced" (the platform) and "Notes" (the bullets). The Wharton letters are the booklet's inserts, and recommendations are printed as the sleeve's critic notes.
- **Research:** four papers as four 7-inch singles, each with its own sleeve.
- **Projects:** a box set of five ventures.
- **Contact:** the back cover: label, catalog line, and booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a 12-inch record, half out of its sleeve, with "Grow the people. The platforms follow." printed on the sleeve in cropped giant type. The label carries my name and "AI/ML Engineering Leader". Hovering or focusing a door drops the needle. Signature interaction: needle drop. The record spins under a tonearm; dropping the needle on a groove band plays that band, meaning it opens that subject. The motion is the tonearm and the platter only.

FORM: grounded candidate 1 of 7 in round 1; seed key 6d3d75b1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
