## Direction contract

THESIS: Engineers recognize a patent figure instantly: precise black line art with reference numerals, leader lines and "FIG. 1". Every system I built is drawn as a figure sheet. Every page is one sheet, one figure, one idea. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: drawing-sheet white (#FCFCFA) and ink (#111111) only, with graphite (#6B6B6B) for construction lines. No color: emphasis comes by inversion, and the active numeral turns white on ink. Type is a typewriter face for claims and descriptions (Courier Prime), with drafting lettering for numerals and "FIG." (Barlow Semi Condensed).

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Services:** "Claims". Each offer is one sheet with its own figure (the 60-minute call as a timing diagram; Fractional CTO as an organization figure), with its price and length.
- **Career:** each chapter is a figure: the Alexa GraphQL aggregation platform, the Flink migration, the Windows 10 Mobile Mail power figure.
- **Speaking:** the keynote as a figure of the stadium.
- **Research:** four papers as four figures. **Projects:** five figures.
- **Contact:** the final sheet, with booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: "Sheet 1 of 7. FIG. 1." A precise isometric line drawing of the media graph: 15+ source blocks feeding one canonical model, which feeds search, recommendations and discovery for 100M+ users. Reference numerals point to each part, and the headline is set as the figure's title. Signature interaction: leader lines. Focusing or hovering a numeral draws its leader line to the part and reveals its description in the margin; figures draw themselves on entry (DrawSVG).
