## Direction contract

THESIS: My practice is rendered as one precise instrument, in the line of Teenage Engineering and Nothing. A single device with a display, a step row and a few controls switches between Hire and Engage. Every page is the same instrument in a different mode. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: a seamless studio sweep (#E6E6E3 to #CFCFCC), an aluminum body (#BDBFC2), black keys (#1B1B1B), signal orange (#FF5A1F) for the active key, and a reflective LCD (#A9B49E with dark segments #1E271A). Silkscreen labels sit on the body. Type is a precise grotesk (Geist) for silkscreen and copy, with a pixel or segment face (Handjet or Sixtyfour) only on the display. The device is rendered in three.js or layered CSS with real light and shadow.

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: the device changes mode, never layout.
- **Services:** the display shows one offer at a time (name, price, length) while the body's panel prints the abstract and proof. The encoder scrubs offers, and the three groups are three mode keys.
- **Speaking:** "live" mode, with a level meter for the Eagles keynote and each Praxtera session as a stored pattern.
- **Career:** the step row expanded, one chapter per step.
- **Research:** four patterns. **Projects:** five patterns.
- **Contact:** the device's output jacks (email, LinkedIn, phone, résumé, book).

FIRST VIEWPORT: the device large and angled in the sweep. Its display reads the headline. Its sixteen-step row is my career, from Carnegie Mellon to Apple, with a chase light running along it. Two physical keys are labeled Engage (Services) and Hire (Résumé). Signature interaction: turn the main encoder (drag, wheel or arrow keys) to scrub the display through facts one at a time; press a step to jump. The chase light runs the step row once on load.
