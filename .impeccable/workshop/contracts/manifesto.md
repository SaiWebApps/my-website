## Direction contract

THESIS: A leadership philosophy deserves a manifesto page set in wood type: words in stacked slabs at right angles, with black rules cutting the field into wedges. "Grow the people. The platforms follow." is the manifesto, and the facts are its proof slabs. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: a puce paper stock (#C99A9E) with one heavy black ink (#141414) and no images. The type mass carries every value. Use enormous condensed wood-type caps (League Gothic, Bowlby One SC, or Big Shoulders Stencil) in unequal slabs, some rotated 90 degrees, split by thick rules into diagonal wedges. Paper grain is fine; never add a second color.

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Services:** each offer is its own manifesto sheet: the name as the slab, the price and length as rules, and the abstract as a justified block.
- **Career:** each chapter is a broadside. **Speaking:** the keynote poster. **Research:** four tracts.
- **Projects:** five sheets. **Contact:** the final proclamation, with booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: "GROW THE PEOPLE" stacked in slabs, with "THE PLATFORMS FOLLOW" rotated up the right edge. Thick rules wedge in my name, title, and the two doors. Signature interaction: slabs slam into place, a press-proof stamp with no bounce, and the rules cut in. Hover re-inks a slab.
