## Direction contract

THESIS: Every chapter and every offer is a box on an archive wall. You read the end labels, pull one box halfway, and lift the lid, and the tissue folds back on the full story. Refuses the category default: a dark hero, a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel and a contact block, plus inner pages that fall back to lists.

OWN-WORLD: burnt-orange box board (#D07A3A), ink (#0D0D0D), tissue white (#F6F6F6), and colorway chips (forest #56734D, steel #4A6B8A, violet #6B4D8E, brick #B13A2E) as state, with a kraft and corrugated texture. Type is condensed caps (Barlow Condensed) on end labels over mono size runs (Kode Mono). End-label anatomy is fixed: name, "colorway" (role or group), "size run" (dates or price and length).

STORY: A hiring leader understands within seconds that I lead people and production AI at Apple scale, believes it from the sourced proof on every screen, and opens the résumé from any page. A buyer understands each offer, its price and its length, believes it from the matching proof, and books an Architecture Call. Every page carries its full inventory as this world's own structure, one vivid subject per screen, never a list. Page concepts: - **Services:** ten boxes, each end label showing name, price and length.
- **Career:** boxes stacked by era, with Apple as the newest box on top.
- **Speaking:** the Eagles keynote box is the oversized "limited release".
- **Research:** four boxes. **Projects:** five boxes.
- **Contact:** the shipping label.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the wall, with one box already pulled halfway. Its lid lifts to show "Grow the people. The platforms follow." folded in tissue, with my name on the end label. Signature interaction: pull, lid hinge, tissue lift, done with CSS 3D. One box is open at a time, and the wall never shows open rows.

FORM: catalog challenger textiles-weave-drape-fashion-sneaker-box-stacks in round 1, verdict competitive; seed key 6d3d75b1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
