# World workshop: sairambkrishnan.com (replacement world)

Mechanism: the engineers Sairam grows ship the AI platforms (Apple's media graph for 100M+ users; Alexa's $40M-a-year platform), and he teaches executives to do the same.
Audience scene: a hiring VP at a frontier lab opens the link from a recruiter note at a desk between interviews; a CEO or event organizer opens it on a phone after a LinkedIn post or a talk.
Cultural home: Seattle/Bay Area engineering leadership, executive education (Wharton), the keynote stage, Apple's media world (music, credits).
First surface must prove: he leads people and platforms at the highest level, and each audience's door is obvious.
Rut (category default): dark hero with name, title and "I lead...", a big-number stat strip, an experience timeline list, a logo row, a testimonial carousel, a contact CTA.
Rut (predictable opposite): a bare type-only page that is a list of links (rauchg / rsms), or cream editorial serif with terracotta.
Brief's own picture: "Grow the people. The platforms follow." Literal reading (garden, growth) gets one candidate at most.

## Grounded candidates, ordered by resonance
1. Liner notes and end credits: album credits (he built the graph behind Apple Music Credits) and film end titles, a form whose whole job is crediting the people who made the work. Family: print packaging / cinema titles.
2. The keynote stage: dark stage, one slide, one sentence, one lit object (Eagles keynote at Lincoln Financial Field, Apple keynotes). Each page is an act with one subject. Family: light / theatre.
3. The design-review whiteboard: marker architecture diagrams that draw themselves, the ritual where engineering leaders decide. Every system shown as its living diagram. Family: hand-drawn / marker.
4. The transit map (Vignelli, Beck): career and offers as colored lines and stations; four offices as interchanges. Two audiences, two lines. Family: graphic wayfinding system.
5. The studio instrument (Teenage Engineering, Nothing, a mixing console): the practice rendered as a precise device whose display and controls switch between Hire and Engage. Family: industrial hardware.
6. The coaching tree (NFL coaching trees, Eagles playbooks): mentorship lineage drawn like the trees that track assistants who became head coaches. Family: sports diagram.
7. The espalier garden (literal reading): people trained along a platform trellis, growing by season. Family: botanical.

## Reserve (later hands)
8. The case packet (HBS / Wharton case method): case, exhibits, teaching note. Family: academic print.
9. The Swiss poster (Muller-Brockmann): each page one commanding poster on a strict grid. Family: typographic poster.
10. The orchestral score (Apple Music Classical): staff systems, a part for each engineer, the conductor's page. Family: notation.
11. The live sentence (Ryo Lu): one sentence that rewrites itself through the facts. Family: kinetic type.
