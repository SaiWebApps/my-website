Ondoway

A local storyteller in your pocket that adapts as you walk.

Co-founder & Chief Technology Officer · AI travel companion · Private beta · Concurrent with Apple

I own product strategy, AI architecture, and end-to-end engineering in the founding team. I designed, architected, coded, and operate the whole system.

Walking tours rarely know where a listener is, what they care about, or how much time they have.

A good tour ties accurate stories to the exact corner in front of you, your interests, and your walking constraints.

A provenance-aware Neo4j knowledge graph grounds every story.

A deterministic tour engine plans and sequences the route, and GPS triggers narration at each stop.

FastAPI backend, authoring tools, and a Flutter mobile app.

Private beta with early testers, including Wharton Executive MBA peers, in New York City and Paris, the first launch city.

I turned their feedback into route-sequencing priorities.

Every spoken sentence traces back to a sourced beat.

I evaluate with frozen human-labeled cases, blinded comparisons, evidence checks, and code-enforced gates.

I rejected a cheaper LLM judge after calibration exposed false rejections of valid paraphrases, separating model-quality failures from truncation before making the cost decision.

I instrument model and prompt identity, tokens, latency, responses, evidence, and the cost of every generation and narration, failed attempts included, to guide debugging, model selection, and unit economics.

Persuaider

Practice, simulate, and evaluate the hard conversation.

Creator & AI Engineering Lead · AI negotiation simulation and coaching · Independent work

I built the complete product: agent behavior, multiagent simulation, evaluation, and its multi-provider AI operating model.

Negotiation training is hard to personalize, repeat, and evaluate consistently.

Practice needs credible competing interests and feedback grounded in what was actually said and agreed.

Scenario authoring and in-character AI personas with distinct motivations, confidential objectives, and walk-away limits.

Interactive practice and stateful AI-versus-AI negotiations.

LLMs extract the negotiated terms, then code scores utility, deal feasibility, walk-away, and value left on the table against fixed rules.

Production-grade application with persisted transcripts and usage, security controls, transparent model failover, and a deep automated test suite.

I score negotiation economics separately from conversational skill.

Coaching follows configurable, weighted, framework-based rubrics, and every quotation and number in it is checked against the transcript in code, returned for bounded repair, and withheld if it still fails.

Gestiq

Deliberate desktop actions from hand motion.

Creator · On-device computer vision · Independent work

I designed and built the native macOS product, extending my Carnegie Mellon capstone: a 2048 puzzle game played with hand gestures.

Camera input is uncertain.

Useful gesture control must tell an intentional action from jitter, tracking loss, and the hand returning to rest.

Apple Vision hand-pose estimation runs on device.

Temporal recognition normalizes motion to palm size, which makes two-hand controls possible and routes commands by the active application or website.

Working macOS application with a SwiftUI interface and menu-bar controls.

Video never leaves the Mac.

Confidence, timing, and target identity travel through the whole perception-to-action path.

The app processes only the latest frame, rechecks the target at the final dispatch, and shows live diagnostics, so uncertain camera input never fires a desktop action.

Lore / DependIQ

The verification layer AI coding agents lack.

I designed the product, the graph model, the algorithms, and the senior-engineer audit.

Repository-local tooling cannot explain the organization-wide blast radius of a dependency change.

PostgreSQL holds identity and history, Neo4j traverses dependencies, and task-aware routing sends each job to a hosted or local model.

Working multi-tenant product with more than 200 tests.

AI-assisted engineering needs explicit dependency truth before it needs another generator.

Crucible

Engineering workflows that make AI agents show their work.

I turn engineering judgment into reusable skills, agent perspectives, hooks, adversarial review, and executable acceptance rules.

An agent’s confident report is weak evidence that a change is correct, complete, or recoverable, and prompt-only guidance is easy for an agent to ignore when delivery pressure rises.

Twelve skills and four pattern guides built on structured agent reports, claim-level evidence checks, risk-based verification, mutation and undo evidence, concurrent review, deterministic gates, and bounded revision loops.

Open-source toolkit with runtime hooks and regression tests, and the working counterpart to my management research.

Separately, my internal agent-engineering work supports daily operations and was adopted and extended by QA, Operations, and upstream and downstream teams.

Reliable agents come from executable constraints.

I keep model judgment separate from the code that checks evidence structure, enforces workflow decisions, and controls when work can proceed.
