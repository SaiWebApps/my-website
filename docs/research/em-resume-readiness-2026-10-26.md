## What these employers repeatedly reward

- **Real people leadership:** hiring, performance management, coaching, growth, and accountability—not merely project coordination. The Apple and Amazon openings already make this explicit.
- **Hands-on technical authority:** architecture, design reviews, complex implementation, and operational debugging remain central even at manager level.
- **Roadmap and product judgment:** prioritization, sequencing, user/customer discovery, and cross-functional influence recur across all four companies.
- **Production discipline:** evaluation, observability, availability, rollout safety, incident learning, and platform leverage are recurring signals.
- **Applied AI is a valid leadership lane:** OpenAI Cooperative Systems, Anthropic Claude Platform, Anthropic Research Data Platform, Snowflake's platform roles, and Google AI Compute do not require personally pretraining frontier models. The résumé's graph grounding, evaluation, agent controls, data systems, and product feedback loops are therefore core strengths, not substitutes to apologize for.
- **Post-training is role-dependent:** OpenAI Search requires close partnership with Post-Training; the other seven audited roles do not establish weight-level post-training as a universal EM requirement.

## Necessary changes to the master résumé

**None.** It already exposes the recurring evidence: eight-engineer leadership, senior-engineer development, applied ML, LLM evaluation, graph grounding, agent systems, distributed platforms, operational improvements, scale, and business outcomes.

Tailor emphasis per application without adding unsupported technologies:

- **OpenAI Cooperative / Anthropic Claude Platform:** lead with Crucible, applied-AI automation, customer/internal-user loops, hands-on architecture, and people development.
- **Anthropic Research Data Platform:** foreground canonical graph/data contracts, curation, human labels/corrections, entity resolution, and SIG.
- **Google AI Agent:** foreground graph-grounded evaluation, HITL corrections, evaluator calibration, and agent evidence gates; confirm and state relevant ML/GenAI durations only if factually supportable.
- **Snowflake Bedrock / AI-Native Authoring:** foreground production platform scale, developer tooling, reliability, roadmap sequencing, hands-on leadership, and language breadth.
- **OpenAI Search:** apply only with a candid account of adjacent retrieval/evaluation experience; do not claim direct search ranking, indexing, Post-Training ownership, or A/B experimentation without evidence.

Do **not** add PyTorch/TensorFlow, fine-tuning, RL pipelines, model distillation, GCP/Spanner, Kubernetes, search ranking, or a formal Engineering Manager title unless new evidence supports each claim.
